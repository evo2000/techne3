technology

techne.online is built with a custom static-site generator, written in PHP. Once the site grew to the point where pure HTML/CSS became cumbersome and error-prone, I looked into various web frameworks and static-site generators. I was discouraged by the many dependencies and complexities that went along with them, when I mostly just needed a templating engine. I settled on the home-built option, dubbed techne-ssg.
